The Cosmic Calendar

13.8 Billion Years Compressed Into One Year

Every month = ~1.15 billion years

Every day = ~38 million years

Every hour = ~1.6 million years

Every second = ~438 years

January 1, 12:00 AM

The Big Bang

13.8 billion years ago

The universe begins. Space and time itself come into existence. All matter and energy compressed into an infinitesimally small point explodes outward.

January 10

First Stars Ignite

13.5 billion years ago

The first stars form from clouds of hydrogen and helium. They are massive, short-lived, and create heavier elements when they explode as supernovae.

January 22

First Galaxies

13.2 billion years ago

Gravity pulls stars together into the first galaxies. These early galaxies are smaller and more chaotic than modern galaxies.

March 15

Milky Way Forms

~11 billion years ago

Our home galaxy begins to take shape through mergers of smaller galaxies. It will continue growing and evolving for billions of years.

September - Our Solar System Forms

September 2

Solar System Forms

4.6 billion years ago

A cloud of gas and dust collapses to form our Sun and the planets. We are made of stardust from previous generations of stars.

September 6

Earth Forms

4.5 billion years ago

Earth coalesces from the debris orbiting the young Sun. It's a molten hellscape, constantly bombarded by asteroids and comets.

September 14

The Moon Forms

4.4 billion years ago

A Mars-sized object crashes into Earth. The debris coalesces to form our Moon, which stabilizes Earth's rotation and climate.

September 21

First Life on Earth

3.8 billion years ago

The first single-celled organisms appear in Earth's oceans. Life begins as simple bacteria and archaea.

October 31

Oxygen Revolution

2.4 billion years ago

Cyanobacteria begin producing oxygen through photosynthesis. This "Great Oxidation Event" transforms Earth's atmosphere and enables complex life.

December - Complex Life Emerges

December 5

First Multicellular Life

1.2 billion years ago

Cells begin working together. The first multicellular organisms are simple algae and sponge-like creatures.

December 17

Cambrian Explosion

541 million years ago

Life explodes in diversity. Most major animal groups appear within 20 million years. Eyes, shells, and predation evolve.

December 20

Land Plants Appear

470 million years ago

Plants colonize land, transforming barren continents into green landscapes. They pave the way for animals to follow.

December 21

Insects Take Flight

400 million years ago

The first insects evolve wings. Some dragonfly ancestors have wingspans of 2.5 feet.

December 26, 6:00 AM

Dinosaurs Appear

230 million years ago

The first dinosaurs evolve. They will dominate Earth for 165 million years.

December 27

First Mammals

200 million years ago

Tiny shrew-like mammals emerge. They live in the shadow of dinosaurs, mostly active at night.

December 30, 6:00 AM

Flowering Plants

130 million years ago

Flowers evolve, creating partnerships with insects and transforming ecosystems.

December 30, 11:00 PM

Dinosaurs Go Extinct

66 million years ago

A 10km asteroid strikes Earth. 75% of all species go extinct, including all non-avian dinosaurs. Mammals inherit the Earth.

December 31 - The Final Day

All of human history happens in the final minutes

December 31, 6:00 AM

Primates Evolve

55 million years ago

Early primates appear, tree-dwelling mammals with forward-facing eyes and grasping hands.

December 31, 11:52 PM

First Humans (Homo habilis)

2.5 million years ago

The first species of genus Homo appears in Africa. They use stone tools.

December 31, 11:58:43 PM

Homo sapiens Emerges

300,000 years ago

Modern humans evolve in Africa. We are the last surviving human species.

December 31, 11:59:46 PM

Agriculture Begins

10,000 years ago

Humans domesticate plants and animals. Civilization begins. Cities, writing, and complex societies emerge.

December 31, 11:59:56 PM

Ancient Civilizations

5,000 years ago

Egypt, Mesopotamia, Indus Valley, and China develop writing, mathematics, and architecture.

December 31, 11:59:59 PM

Modern Era

Last ~500 years

The Renaissance, Scientific Revolution, Industrial Revolution, and Digital Age all happen in the final second of the cosmic year.

December 31, 11:59:59.99 PM

Right Now

Present day

You are here. Reading this. The universe has been around for 13.8 billion years, and recorded human history spans less than one second on the cosmic calendar.

Putting It in Perspective

If you started January 1st...

And waited all year for humans to appear, you'd have to stay awake until 8 minutes before midnight on New Year's Eve.

All of recorded history

Every empire, every war, every discovery, every person who has ever lived— it all happens in the last 14 seconds of the year.

Your entire lifetime

A human lifespan of 80 years = about 0.018 seconds on the cosmic calendar. A fraction of a blink.

Dinosaurs ruled for...

165 million years = about 4.5 days. Humans have existed for less than 8 minutes.
