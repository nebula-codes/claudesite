# The Explorer's Hub 🌊🌌🧠

An interactive educational web experience featuring three distinct journeys of discovery:

## 🌊 Deep Ocean Mysteries
Descend through the ocean's depth zones from the sunlit surface to the crushing darkness of the Mariana Trench. Discover bizarre creatures, mysterious sounds, and the alien world beneath the waves.

**Features:**
- Interactive depth tracking with pressure indicators
- 5 ocean zones from Sunlight to Hadal
- Real creature facts and adaptations
- Particle effects and bioluminescence simulation
- Mysterious ocean sounds information (The Bloop, Julia, etc.)

## 🌌 The Cosmic Calendar
Experience all 13.8 billion years of cosmic history compressed into a single calendar year. See how recent humanity really is.

**Features:**
- Interactive timeline of major cosmic events
- Big Bang to present day
- Perspective comparisons showing the vastness of time
- Starfield background with shooting stars
- Navigation to jump between key events
- Every month = ~1.15 billion years!

## 🧠 Optical Illusions Lab
Explore how your brain tricks you with 8 interactive optical illusions. Each includes scientific explanations and interactive controls.

**Illusions Included:**
1. Hermann Grid - Phantom gray dots
2. Rotating Snakes - Static image that moves
3. Checker Shadow - Same colors look different
4. Café Wall - Parallel lines appear tilted
5. Ebbinghaus - Relative size perception
6. Motion Aftereffect - Waterfall illusion
7. Penrose Triangle - Impossible geometry
8. Color Constancy - Red strawberries with no red pixels

## 🚀 Technology

Built with pure vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3:
